Unit 2 Comparative Matrix

Paleolithic Era vs. Neolithic Era

Feature Paleolithic (Old Stone Age) Neolithic (New Stone Age)
Food Source Hunted wild game, fished, and foraged wild berries, nuts, and honey (unpredictable supply). Domesticated wheat, barley, peas, figs, goats, sheep, cattle, and pigs; created a food surplus.
Settlement Nomadic extended-family bands (<100 people) moving across 7–500 sq. mi.; used caves & camps. Permanent mud-brick settlements near Fertile Crescent rivers; villages grew into towns.
Human-Environment Interaction ADAPTING to nature: wearing animal skins and sheltering in natural limestone caves. MODIFYING nature: clearing land and digging irrigation canals to water crops.
Tools & Jobs Chipped stone hand axes, spears, bone needles, bows; everyone searched for food. Polished stone plows, sickles, pottery; job specialization (potters, weavers, toolmakers).
